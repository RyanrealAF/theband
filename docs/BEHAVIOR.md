# Performer behavior rules

## Ghost piano

The ghost is restrained by default. Piano velocity moves the hands and keys. Sustains hold the hands. Chords illuminate multiple key groups. Large accents increase ghost opacity and disturb the clouds. The ghost should never bob continuously with volume.

## Guitar

Picking responds to note attacks. Fretting responds to pitch. Bends become exaggerated neck/string motion. Strong phrases can trigger sparks, but sustained notes should not spam particles.

## Drums

Kick, snare, hat, and cymbal regions should eventually become separate event channels. Fills can trigger coordinated body movement. Major hits can throw loose trash or rattle the shopping cart.

## Bass

Bass gets the fewest visible gestures and the biggest physical consequences. Sustains make the creature breathe. Attacks pulse the dumpster body. Sub-heavy events move pavement and camera. Drops should be rare and significant.

## World

The world is the fifth performer. It responds to aggregate musical intensity, not every individual note. Low end controls physical weight. High-frequency energy controls sparks, dust, and small light sources. Full-band peaks control large environmental moments.
